What can and can't go in a skip in Taree

In short: What you can and can't put in a skip bin in Taree: general, green and heavy waste, banned items like asbestos and gas bottles, and where to take problem waste locally.

What goes in the bin changes the price and whether the operator can take it. Here's what's usually accepted, what isn't, and where Taree households can take problem waste instead.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Usually accepted

  • General household waste: furniture, toys, clothes, boxes and general junk.
  • Green waste: branches, palm fronds, lawn clippings, camphor laurel prunings and storm debris. A green-only bin is often cheaper than mixed waste, so keep soil and rubbish out of it.
  • Renovation waste: timber, plasterboard, old cabinets, carpet, tiles and fittings (but not fibro that may contain asbestos).
  • Heavy or clean fill: soil, sand, concrete, bricks and pavers, usually in a smaller bin with a weight limit.
  • Scrap metal: old roofing iron, fencing, farm gates and outdoor furniture.

Not accepted, or extra charge

  • Asbestos and fibro: never in a general skip. See below.
  • Tyres: usually banned or charged per tyre.
  • Batteries: car and household batteries are a fire risk in a skip.
  • Gas bottles, fire extinguishers and aerosols: can explode when compacted.
  • Paint, oils, chemicals and liquids: including pool chemicals, sump oil and farm chemicals.
  • Mattresses: often accepted for an extra fee.
  • E-waste, food waste and medical waste: check first. Many operators won't take TVs, computers or fluorescent tubes.

Every operator has its own list. The one who quotes you will confirm theirs, so mention anything unusual in the form.

Asbestos and fibro in Taree

Plenty of older homes, sheds and holiday fibros around Taree, Old Bar and Harrington were built with fibro sheeting, and it can contain asbestos. You can't tell by looking, so if a sheet might be fibro, treat it as asbestos. In NSW, SafeWork NSW says you must use a licensed professional to remove any friable asbestos or more than 10 m² of bonded (non-friable) asbestos, and it recommends a licensed professional for any amount. Asbestos must be wrapped and taken to a facility licensed to accept it, never hidden in a general skip. Asbestos must go to a facility licensed to take it, so call MidCoast Council on (02) 7955 7777 before taking any to a waste centre.

Where to take problem waste

The Taree Waste Management Centre is at 6843 The Bucketts Way, Tinonee. MidCoast Council lists it as open daily from 7am to 5pm (closed Christmas Day and Good Friday), phone (02) 6551 5266. It has a Community Recycling Centre for household problem waste such as paint, gas bottles, batteries and oils, and a Reviva tip shop for reusable goods. Check MidCoast Council's website for current fees and accepted items before you go.

Tips for a cheaper, easier skip

  • Separate green waste and heavy waste from general rubbish where you can.
  • Break down furniture and flatten boxes so they take less space.
  • Don't fill above the rim. Operators can refuse to lift an overfilled bin.
  • Be upfront about mattresses, tyres or anything you're unsure about.
