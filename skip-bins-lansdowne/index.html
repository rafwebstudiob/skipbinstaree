Skip hire in Lansdowne

Lansdowne is a rural village in the Lansdowne River valley north-west of Taree, next to Coorabakh National Park and the Lansdowne State Forests. It's often called the gateway to the Manning's northern hinterland, and has a general store and post office, a primary school and a bowling and recreation club. Skip jobs here are often shed and acreage clean-ups, house clear-outs, renovations and green waste. A 6–9 m³ skip suits most shed or yard jobs.

Access on rural blocks

Mention long or gravel driveways, gates and soft ground in the form, and pick a firm, level spot the truck can reach. Lansdowne is about 20 km from Taree by road, so ask whether delivery distance affects the price.

Nearest tip

There's no council waste centre in Lansdowne. MidCoast Council's closest is the Taree Waste Management Centre at 6843 The Bucketts Way, Tinonee, south-west of Taree. Council lists it as open daily 7am to 5pm, closed Christmas Day and Good Friday, with a Community Recycling Centre for problem waste. It's also one of only two MidCoast sites that take asbestos (the other is Gloucester), and council asks you to complete its application to dispose of asbestos first. For a big clean-out, a skip saves the round trips.

Council rules

A skip wholly on your own land generally doesn't need approval. On the road, footpath or verge, check with MidCoast Council first. See the permit guide.
