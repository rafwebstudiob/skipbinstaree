Skip hire in Cundletown

Cundletown is a village on the north-east side of Taree and home to Taree Regional Airport on Lansdowne Road. It's close enough to Taree that most local operators deliver here easily. Jobs range from house clean-outs and renovations to sheds and small business clean-ups.

Floods and low ground

Parts of the Manning floodplain around Cundletown are low-lying. After a flood, wet carpet, plasterboard and furniture pile up quickly and are heavy. Choose "Mixed, not sure" or describe the job, and ask for a larger bin and a quick pick-up if you need it. Put the skip on the firmest ground you have.

Nearest tip

The Taree Waste Management Centre at 6843 The Bucketts Way, Tinonee, is open daily 7am to 5pm (closed Christmas Day and Good Friday). It has a licensed landfill, weighbridge, Community Recycling Centre and Reviva tip shop.

Council rules

On your own land a skip generally doesn't need approval. On the road or footpath, check with MidCoast Council. See the permit guide.
