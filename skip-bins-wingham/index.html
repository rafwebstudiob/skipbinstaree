Skip hire in Wingham

Wingham is a historic town on the Manning River, known for Wingham Brush Nature Reserve, a patch of riverside rainforest that's a major roosting site for grey-headed flying-foxes. Around town there are plenty of older weatherboard homes, and beyond it rural blocks and farms. That means a mix of renovation jobs, deceased-estate clear-outs and shed or farm clean-ups.

A 4 m³ skip suits a bathroom or laundry, and 6–8 m³ a kitchen or whole-house clear-out. Farm clean-ups with old fencing and roofing iron often need 9 m³ or more.

Nearest tip

There's no MidCoast Council waste centre in Wingham itself. The closest is the Taree Waste Management Centre at 6843 The Bucketts Way, Tinonee, open daily 7am to 5pm (closed Christmas Day and Good Friday). It has a Community Recycling Centre for problem waste and a Reviva tip shop. Wingham Library is one of council's drop-off points for household batteries, mobile phones and printer cartridges.

Fibro in older homes

Many older Manning Valley homes and sheds have fibro, which can contain asbestos. It can't go in a general skip. If you're renovating, say "May contain asbestos" in the form. See what can go in a skip.

Council rules

On your own land, a skip generally doesn't need approval. On the street or footpath, check with MidCoast Council first. See the permit guide.
